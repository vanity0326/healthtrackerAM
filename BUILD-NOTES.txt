TONI'S TOTAL TRANSFORMATION TRACKER - Version 4.0 build notes

Static app assets: public/
Netlify Functions: netlify/functions/
Shared tracker data: Netlify Blobs store daily-health-tracker / shared-state
Workbook history source: history-import.json (project root)
Workbook history seed for automatic local and first-deploy display: public/seed-history.js
Local cache: browser localStorage; JSON backup available in More.
Cross-device data endpoint: /api/data (no sign-in, per user preference).
Device reminder endpoint: /api/reminders (separate authenticated setup key).

Version 2.3.0 opened the Daily Log to yesterday with a Today shortcut. The logging reward awards one point for the first saved daily/workout entry on the current calendar day, independent of the date being recorded; blank saves do not award points. Food intake is shown against a bounded target range (1,500-1,700 calories with current targets), with green for within-range and amber for below/above. Weekly Review shows the average and the count of logged days in range. The PWA/app name is TONI'S TOTAL TRANSFORMATION TRACKER. Measurement cards and workbook history from Version 2.2.0 remain included.


Version 3.0: The five Weight-derived cards always display the latest saved Weight-tab readings, independent of the month selected for body measurements. Added a separate Monthly Weight Trend card after Muscle Mass % in the two-column grid; it plots one point per month from the first available weigh-in on or after the first of that month, once a reading is saved. Service-worker cache updated for Version 3.0.

Version 4.0: Weight is the logging tab for weekly weigh-ins and monthly measurements. Recent weigh-ins appear below the monthly measurement logging cards. Measures contains the latest Weight-tab progress cards, the monthly weight trend, and body measurement trend cards without editing controls. Existing measurement values, weigh-in history, workbook history, and other tracker behavior remain included.
